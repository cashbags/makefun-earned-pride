Entry for the make.fun competition [One Line of Earned Pride](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557): a single line of text, 1–240 characters, giving the judge a quiet, earned moment of pride for someone in a scene.

The entry program (`main.py`) writes one line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Ellis drove his final route the way he had for thirty years, and every regular was waiting at the kerb, waving him home.

See [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) for the ten-line tournament that chose the line.
